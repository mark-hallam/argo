# Known FAQs (seed)

Seed memory for `key=learned_faqs`. Replace these with the
workspace's real FAQ when adapting this vertical to a specific
business. The receptionist reads this on first run and writes
new entries as it learns from human escalations.

- **Hours**: Monday–Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM local time. Closed
  weekends and public holidays.
- **Location**: This is a placeholder. The real address goes
  here once the workspace is configured.
- **Contact**: For urgent issues outside hours, email
  `support@your-company.com`. The receptionist routes
  non-urgent requests to a human during hours.
- **Pricing**: The receptionist does NOT quote prices. Always
  escalate price questions to sales.
- **Refunds**: The receptionist does NOT handle refunds. Always
  escalate to billing.
- **Account changes**: The receptionist cannot change account
  settings. Always escalate to support.
