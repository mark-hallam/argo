# Data Processing Addendum (template)

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the SKYCOT Terms of Service ("Agreement") between SKYCOT ("Processor") and the customer ("Controller") and governs the processing of Personal Data by SKYCOT on the Controller's behalf in connection with the SKYCOT service.

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement, the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the UK GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act, as applicable.

- **Personal Data** — information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed by SKYCOT on behalf of the Controller.
- **Processing** — any operation performed on Personal Data, automated or not.
- **Subprocessor** — any third party engaged by SKYCOT to process Personal Data on the Controller's behalf.
- **Data Subject** — the individual to whom Personal Data relates.

## 2. Roles and scope

The Controller is the controller of Personal Data; SKYCOT acts as Processor. SKYCOT processes Personal Data only:

- on documented instructions from the Controller (including those embodied in the Agreement and the Controller's use of the service);
- as required by law applicable to SKYCOT, in which case SKYCOT informs the Controller of the legal requirement before processing (unless prohibited by law).

The subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, types of Personal Data, and categories of Data Subjects are described in Annex A.

## 3. Confidentiality

SKYCOT ensures that personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations (contractually or by statute).

## 4. Security

SKYCOT implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including those described in Annex B (the "Security Measures"). SKYCOT may update the Security Measures, provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

## 5. Subprocessing

The Controller authorizes SKYCOT to engage Subprocessors to process Personal Data subject to this DPA. A current list of Subprocessors is available at [skycot.com/legal/subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors). SKYCOT will:

- impose data-protection terms on each Subprocessor no less protective than this DPA;
- remain responsible for each Subprocessor's compliance;
- give the Controller at least 30 days' notice of any new Subprocessor, during which the Controller may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

## 6. Data Subject rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, SKYCOT will assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under applicable law (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, automated-decision-making).

If SKYCOT receives a request directly from a Data Subject relating to Personal Data processed for the Controller, SKYCOT will forward the request to the Controller without undue delay and will not respond except on the Controller's instructions or as required by law.

## 7. Personal data breach

SKYCOT will notify the Controller without undue delay (and in any event within 72 hours where feasible) after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data, and will provide the Controller with information reasonably necessary to comply with its breach notification obligations.

## 8. Data Protection Impact Assessments

SKYCOT will provide reasonable assistance to the Controller with any Data Protection Impact Assessment or prior consultation with supervisory authorities that the Controller is required to carry out.

## 9. International transfers

Where SKYCOT transfers Personal Data outside the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two — controller to processor) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, which are incorporated into this DPA by reference and completed in Annex C.

## 10. Return or deletion

On termination of the Agreement, SKYCOT will, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all Personal Data and delete existing copies, unless retention is required by law. Backups are deleted in accordance with our standard retention schedule.

## 11. Audits

The Controller may audit SKYCOT's compliance with this DPA on 30 days' notice, no more than once per twelve-month period (or more frequently where required by a supervisory authority or after a Personal Data Breach). Audits will be conducted during business hours, subject to confidentiality, and limited to information necessary to verify compliance.

SKYCOT may satisfy audit obligations by providing copies of relevant third-party certifications (e.g., SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001) where available.

## 12. Liability and precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Agreement. If there is any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA prevails in respect of the processing of Personal Data.

## Annex A — Description of processing

- **Subject matter**: provision of the SKYCOT AI-worker platform.
- **Duration**: the term of the Agreement plus the retention period set out in the privacy policy.
- **Nature and purpose**: hosting, executing, and auditing AI workers configured by the Controller; routing model traffic to providers selected by the Controller.
- **Categories of Personal Data**: identifiers (name, email), professional information, content provided to workers, action logs.
- **Categories of Data Subjects**: Controller personnel; individuals whose information is provided to workers by the Controller.

## Annex B — Security measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
- Connector credentials stored in a key-management service with audited access.
- Principle of least privilege; access to customer data is logged.
- Quarterly access reviews.
- Vulnerability management programme.
- Personnel security training on data protection and information security.
- Business continuity and incident response procedures.

## Annex C — Standard Contractual Clauses

The parties incorporate by reference the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module Two — controller to processor) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Module-specific options (governing law, supervisory authority, etc.) are completed in the signature pages of this DPA.
