# Marketing Intern Pack

The Marketing Intern vertical supports a growth team with repeatable content and campaign operations. It drafts long-form and social content from approved positioning, prepares analytics readouts from funnel data, coordinates scheduled posts, and monitors competitor changes for review.

## Skills

- `draft_blog`: turns a campaign brief and evidence packet into a structured blog draft with claims, proof points, and review notes.
- `draft_social`: adapts approved campaign messages into channel-specific posts and flags risky claims.
- `analytics_report`: summarizes UTM, funnel, and channel metrics against baselines with anomaly notes.
- `schedule_post`: prepares a calendar-ready publishing plan and routes approval for externally visible posts.
- `competitive_monitor`: captures competitor changes, classifies severity, and proposes response options.

## Operating Boundaries

The worker may draft and analyze autonomously, but publication, spend changes, pricing claims, and competitor-response messaging require operator approval. The pack assumes enterprise channels such as CMS, LinkedIn, calendar, analytics, and CRM are exposed through MCP tools.
