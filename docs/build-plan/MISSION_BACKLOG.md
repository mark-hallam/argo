## Source rationale

The deep-research analysis returned the following honest assessment
(captured verbatim for the record so future readers see the framing):

> Substrate (primitives): ~75% complete.
> Runtime (closed loops): ~45% complete.
> Mission spirit: ~45% delivered.
>
> The gap is enumerable, scoped, and almost entirely tractable —
> there are no architectural dead-ends visible. The two structural
> risks (no CI; no gateway auth) are 1-day and 1-week fixes. The
> one strategic risk (others ship 19 messaging platforms; you have
> 3 in-tree) is a multi-month catch-up that is parallelizable with
> mission closure.
