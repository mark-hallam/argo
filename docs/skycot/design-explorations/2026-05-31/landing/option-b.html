01 provable by design — the hash-chained ledger

Every action, provable.

Every action a teammate takes is written to a hash-chained, tamper-evident ledger — each row carries the hash of the one before it, so altering or deleting history breaks the chain and is detectable. No researched competitor ships cryptographic chained audit.

02 the truth boundary — claim-level gating

An AI that knows what it's not allowed to say.

A claim-level truth boundary gates what a teammate may assert — not just what it can access. Portia, our pitch agent, can't state a number she can't back up. She defers to a human instead. This appears to be a category we own: everyone else controls access; we control what the agent is allowed to say.

portia · pitch session · truth_boundary=on
prospect> what's your churn rate?
portia> ⚠ claim "churn = N%" has no backing source in scope.
portia> "I'm not able to quote a churn figure I can't substantiate — let me bring in a human who can."
