01Provable by design

Every action, provable.

Every action a teammate takes is written to a hash-chained, tamper-evident ledger — cryptographically impossible to alter or delete after the fact. The closest thing rivals offer is access-control and anomaly detection, not a provable record of what actually happened.

An audit trail you can take to your board — or your regulator.

02The truth boundary

An AI that knows what it's not allowed to say.

A claim-level truth boundary gates what a teammate may assert — not just what it can access. Our pitch agent literally cannot state a number she can't back up; she defers to a human instead. Everyone else controls access. We control what the agent is allowed to say.

Brand-safe and legally-safe by construction — not by prompt-wishing.

Nothing risky without your yes.

Autonomy with a seatbelt. You're always in the loop.
