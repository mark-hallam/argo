04 · BACKGROUND RECIPE

One recipe. Every page.

Warm-white body + two fixed radial blobs. Top-right purple. Bottom-left pink. Both at low opacity. They don't scroll. They're ambient, not decoration.

::before · 700×700 · purple 0.15 ::after · 800×800 · pink 0.10 
body {
  background: var(--bg);  /* #fafaff */
  position: relative;
}
body::before {

07 · VOICE + COPYWRITING

Talk to operators like peers.

SKYCOT's voice is conversational, confident, specific. Worker names in actions. Numbers over vague claims. Time-bounded promises.

DO
  • "DON'T BUY ANOTHER TOOL. HIRE A TEAMMATE."
  • "In four minutes, you'll have hired your first AI teammate."
  • "Nori flagged a 32% discount on QT-2024-7283 — exceeds your 25% threshold."
  • "Jamal resolved SUP-29382 using KB SK-413. Customer sentiment: resolved."
  • "Built in Melbourne."
DON'T
  • "AI for your business"
  • "The future of work is here"
  • "Welcome to SKYCOT!"
  • "Our worker detected an anomaly"
  • "Industry-leading accuracy"
08 · APPLICATION RULES

What's in. What's out.

USE
  • Audiowide for h1 + wordmark on every page
  • Purple → pink gradient on CTAs and brand marks
  • 3-color gradient on h1 strong emphasis
  • #fafaff warm-white body background + fixed radial blobs
  • #1e1b4b purple-tinted ink (not neutral black)
  • Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for technical data
  • Worker-specific avatar gradients consistently
  • Status dots with box-shadow glow on live states
AVOID
  • Purple → cyan gradient (superseded by purple → pink)
  • #18181B for ink (use #1e1b4b)
  • Pure white #FFFFFF body background
  • Audiowide for body copy or labels (display only)
  • The brand gradient stacked on top of dark gradients
  • Audiowide hero below 28px on any surface
  • Recoloring the brand mark to a single solid color
